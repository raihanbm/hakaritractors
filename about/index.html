About Hikari Tractors

Independent tractor spare-parts sourcing, built around model-first catalog navigation.

What we do

Hikari Tractors helps customers identify tractor spare parts through model, system, diagram, and part-number references. Customers can browse the catalog and send an RFQ when fitment, price, stock, freight, or lead time needs confirmation.

Independent supplier notice

Hikari Tractors is an independent supplier of tractor spare parts. Brand names, trademarks, model names, and part numbers are used for product identification purposes only and remain the property of their respective owners. Hikari Tractors is not presented as an official Kubota website, dealer, or authorized representative.

How enquiries work

  1. Start with the exact tractor model, part number, or exploded diagram.
  2. Add required lines to the RFQ cart and provide contact and destination details.
  3. Our team reviews the request and confirms applicable fitment, availability, pricing, freight, and terms before any order is accepted.
