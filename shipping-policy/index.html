Shipping Policy

How freight, delivery estimates, and shipment details are handled for RFQ orders.

Shipping is quote-based

Shipping availability, method, cost, transit estimate, insurance, customs paperwork, duties, taxes, and destination restrictions are confirmed through an RFQ or quotation. The website does not represent an instant checkout or guaranteed delivery service.

What we need

  • Accurate destination and postal code where applicable.
  • Requested parts, quantities, and model/fitment context.
  • Preferred commercial term or delivery preference, if known.

Before dispatch

Dispatch timing starts only after an order is confirmed under agreed commercial terms. Estimated timing may change due to supplier availability, carrier operations, customs, force majeure, or incomplete customer information.

Receiving goods
