Hikari Tractors · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

How Hikari Tractors handles RFQ, support, and website information.

Information we collect

When you submit an RFQ or contact Hikari Tractors, we may collect your name or company name, email address, destination, requested parts, message, tractor/diagram information, and quotation preferences. We also process basic technical information needed to operate and protect the website, such as server logs and rate-limit signals.

Why we use it

  • To prepare, respond to, and manage RFQ enquiries.
  • To check catalog references, fitment context, availability, freight, and quotation terms.
  • To prevent spam, abuse, and service disruption.
  • To meet applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Where it goes

RFQ data is sent to the Hikari Tractors order service and stored in the business backend used to manage enquiries. If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp or email, your message is processed by those services under their own policies. Do not include payment card data, passwords, or other unnecessary sensitive information in RFQ notes.

Retention and choices

RFQ records are retained for [DATA RETENTION PERIOD] or longer where required for legitimate business, legal, accounting, dispute, or anti-fraud purposes. You can request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting info@hikaritractors.com. We may need to verify the request before acting.

Cookies and analytics

This storefront uses browser storage for functional preferences such as language, currency, cart, and saved catalog state. It does not intentionally send raw RFQ names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, or message content to advertising or analytics events. If analytics or marketing tools are added later, this policy must be updated before their use.
