Start with an exact parts reference

Use the catalog to select the tractor model, system, exploded diagram, and the required part lines. Include part numbers, diagram codes, and quantities whenever available. This reduces ambiguity and gives the RFQ reviewer usable fitment context.

Include buyer and destination details

For an RFQ, include your name or company, email or WhatsApp contact, destination city/country, and any preferred trade term. International buyers should state the destination and requested term so freight options can be evaluated during quotation.

What Hikari confirms through an RFQ

RFQs are reviewed before an order is accepted. The response can confirm applicable fitment information, availability, pricing, lead time, freight options, and commercial terms where applicable. Catalog data is a reference and is not a final commercial offer.

Send your request

Frequently asked questions

What information should be included?

Include the tractor model, part number or diagram code, quantities, destination, and any serial range or fitment detail available.

Are price, stock, and freight final before quotation?

No. Availability, fitment, commercial pricing, freight options, lead time, and terms are confirmed through the RFQ process.

Can international buyers submit an RFQ?

Yes. Include the destination and requested trade term. Freight options and commercial terms are reviewed after the request; they are not guaranteed by the catalog alone.
