Warranty

Warranty handling for spare parts supplied through Hikari Tractors.

Warranty review

Warranty coverage, if any, is confirmed in the applicable quotation, supplier documentation, or order terms. Coverage depends on the product, manufacturer policy, installation, usage, maintenance, and evidence provided.

Making a claim

Send the order reference, part number, product photos, installation details, symptoms, and supporting evidence to info@hikaritractors.com. Do not discard the product or packaging until we provide instructions.

Important limits

Catalog references do not create a separate warranty. Hikari Tractors does not make unsupported claims of manufacturer authorization, guaranteed availability, or universal fitment. Warranty outcomes are assessed case by case under the confirmed order terms.
