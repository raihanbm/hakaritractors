WHAT TO SEND

Details needed for an import/export quotation

  • Tractor model: for example L3608, L4400DT, L4018DT-ID/L4018TK-ID, or the model shown on your machine.
  • Part reference: part number, callout, diagram code, or a clear photo of the old part.
  • Quantity: requested purchase quantity and, when useful, the diagram quantity per assembly.
  • Destination: city/country, port or delivery preference, and any urgency.
  • Fitment information: serial range, variant, year, or measurements when the reference has old/new options.
