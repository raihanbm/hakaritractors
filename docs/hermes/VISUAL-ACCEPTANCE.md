# Visual Acceptance Checklist

## Desktop capture conditions

- Viewport: 1672 × 941
- Zoom: 100%
- Device scale factor: 1
- Route at top of page
- Hard refresh after deployment

## Homepage

- black utility bar, white search header, navigation, and model strip all visible;
- hero retains full headline, two actions, tractor image, and floating five-part search panel;
- six model cards in one row;
- seven system cards in one row;
- four featured assembly cards in one row;
- benefits strip and complete dark footer visible without overlapping text.

## Catalog

- left filter rail approximately 218px;
- toolbar and active filters aligned above results;
- four product cards per row;
- every card contains a diagram, title, code, model badge, metadata, price/status, and full View Diagram button;
- card buttons are not clipped.

## Diagram detail

- large diagram panel left and summary/parts panel right;
- diagram toolbar, navigation arrows, thumbnails, download, and bulk-RFQ controls visible;
- parts table remains readable and scrollable;
- Add to RFQ changes to `− quantity + ×` after adding;
- zero quantity removes the part and restores Add to RFQ;
- related assemblies display below the main split.

## Functional regression

- search by model, part number, diagram code, and category;
- model and system filters;
- grid/list control;
- diagram page opening;
- individual and bulk RFQ add;
- plus, minus, remove, and cart badge synchronization;
- refresh persistence;
- CSV/export and RFQ fallback behavior.
